# Crit 7 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

Every test was green, but a new plan with no courses already showed one requirement as met, because "at most 60 units of 1000-level courses" is true when nothing is planned. The logic was right; the meaning was wrong. From then on I asked what a student would conclude from the screen, not just whether a rule evaluated correctly. That led to separating real requirements from limits, and later to rebuilding the page around next steps.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It made me want to be more creative. The agent handled the exact work well: transcribing rules, writing the engine, keeping tests green. What it couldn't do was imagine a better experience. The ideas that improved the planner most were mine: three next steps instead of a dense audit, adding a course straight from search, and undo after every change. I want to spend my energy on inventing what's worth building.
